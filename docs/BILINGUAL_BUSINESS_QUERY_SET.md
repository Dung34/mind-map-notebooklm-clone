# Bo cau query song ngu (VI-EN) de test mindmap phan tich doanh nghiep

Tai lieu nay cung cap 10 cau query song ngu Viet-Anh de test chat luong truy xuat, framework selection va output mindmap cho cac use-case:
- Phan tich doanh nghiep
- He sinh thai nganh
- Du doan phat trien
- Kha nang hop tac

## Cach su dung nhanh

- Neu user khong nhap query, co the dung 1 cau trong bo nay lam default.
- Neu user nhap query rieng, uu tien query user.
- Nen chay test theo nhom chu de va so sanh do on dinh output (node quality, evidence, actionability).

## 10 query song ngu de test

1) **Phan tich nang luc cot loi / Core capability analysis**  
**VI:** Phan tich diem manh, diem yeu cot loi cua doanh nghiep va tac dong den nang luc canh tranh trong 2 nam toi.  
**EN:** Analyze the company's core strengths and weaknesses and their impact on competitive capability over the next two years.

2) **Vi the trong he sinh thai / Ecosystem positioning**  
**VI:** Danh gia vi the doanh nghiep trong he sinh thai nganh, bao gom doi tac, nha cung cap, khach hang va cac phu thuoc chien luoc.  
**EN:** Assess the company's position in the industry ecosystem, including partners, suppliers, customers, and strategic dependencies.

3) **Co hoi tang truong / Growth opportunity mapping**  
**VI:** Xac dinh cac co hoi tang truong kha thi nhat dua tren nang luc hien tai, thi truong muc tieu va xu huong cong nghe.  
**EN:** Identify the most feasible growth opportunities based on current capabilities, target markets, and technology trends.

4) **Kich ban phat trien / Development scenario planning**  
**VI:** Du doan 3 kich ban phat trien cua doanh nghiep (lac quan, co so, than trong) trong 12-24 thang toi.  
**EN:** Forecast three development scenarios for the company (optimistic, base, conservative) over the next 12-24 months.

5) **Rui ro chien luoc / Strategic risk analysis**  
**VI:** Phan tich cac rui ro chien luoc co the lam cham tang truong va de xuat phuong an giam thieu theo muc uu tien.  
**EN:** Analyze strategic risks that may slow growth and propose prioritized mitigation actions.

6) **San sang hop tac quoc te / International partnership readiness**  
**VI:** Danh gia muc do san sang mo rong hop tac voi doi tac quoc te, gom loi the, rao can va dieu kien tien quyet.  
**EN:** Evaluate readiness to expand collaboration with international partners, including advantages, barriers, and prerequisites.

7) **Benchmark doi thu / Competitive benchmarking**  
**VI:** So sanh doanh nghiep voi cac doi thu cung phan khuc ve nang luc van hanh, doi moi san pham va toc do mo rong thi truong.  
**EN:** Benchmark the company against same-segment competitors in operational capability, product innovation, and market expansion speed.

8) **Gia tri trong chuoi he sinh thai / Ecosystem value creation**  
**VI:** Phan tich kha nang tao gia tri cua doanh nghiep trong chuoi lien ket he sinh thai (cong nghe, du lieu, kenh phan phoi, hau mai).  
**EN:** Analyze the company's value creation potential across the ecosystem chain (technology, data, distribution channels, after-sales services).

9) **Doi tac chien luoc tiem nang / Strategic partner targeting**  
**VI:** Xac dinh nhom doi tac chien luoc tiem nang nhat de dong phat trien san pham hoac mo rong thi truong moi.  
**EN:** Identify the most promising strategic partner groups for co-developing products or entering new markets.

10) **Roadmap hop tac 3 giai doan / 3-phase collaboration roadmap**  
**VI:** De xuat roadmap hop tac theo 3 giai doan (ngan han, trung han, dai han) de toi da hoa tang truong ben vung.  
**EN:** Propose a three-phase collaboration roadmap (short-term, mid-term, long-term) to maximize sustainable growth.

## Goi y bo sung cho test

- Gan them bien the query theo nganh (IT outsourcing, fintech, logistics, edtech) de do do nhay cua he thong.
- Theo doi KPI moi run: do phu keyword chien luoc, do ro action items, tinh nhat quan SWOT/Funnel.
- Luu ket qua theo tung query ID (Q01-Q10) de so sanh qua cac lan tuning prompt/retrieval.
